# Sources and comparison scope

The manuscript bibliography is the authoritative citation list.

| Source | Role here |
| --- | --- |
| Ben-Hamou, Peres and Salez, Weighted sampling without replacement | Related weighted-sampling framework; not a substitute for the proved urn comparison |
| Joag-Dev and Proschan, Negative association of random variables | Explains why a general disjoint-coordinate principle does not settle these overlapping occupancies |
| Klenke and Mattner, Stochastic ordering of classical discrete distributions | Scalar ordering context; this paper proves its three-population comparison directly |
| Kuperberg, Sums of singular series along arithmetic progressions and with smooth weights | Distinguishes two local factors from an average of the full singular series |
| Las Vergnas, The Tutte polynomial of a morphism of matroids, part 4 | Classical rank-generating interpretation; no new general matroid invariant claimed |

The two added corollaries are consequences of the displayed arguments; no
additional external theorem is imported. These notes describe the cited
comparison, not a guarantee of bibliographic completeness or priority.
